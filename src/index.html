    Privacy

    iRevive doesn't collect, track or sell anything about you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

    Your Apple ID and files

    You sign in on Apple's own iCloud page, inside the iCloud window. iRevive never sees your password, your 2FA codes, your files or the file list. All it does in that window is tick checkboxes and click the Restore and OK buttons for you. Apple's privacy policy covers that page.

    What stays on your Mac

    • Apple's sign-in cookies, kept in the app's web storage just like Safari would, so you may stay signed in between launches.
    • The log and counters, which live in memory only and are gone when you quit.
    Sign out and clear data Deletes Apple's cookies, cache and saved site data from iRevive. You'll sign in again next time.

    When iRevive goes online

    • icloud.com: the page you're using to restore.
    • backend.gilroy.digital: once at launch, and again if you press Check for updates, to ask whether a newer version exists. It sees your IP address. Nothing about you or your files is sent.
    • GitHub: only while an update is downloading, since the app files are hosted there. GitHub sees your IP address, as it would for any download.
    • Links to Stripe (donations) and gilroy.digital open in your own browser, and only when you click them.

    Feedback and reports

    Nothing is sent unless you fill in the Feedback form and press Submit. It then goes to Gilroy Digital's ticket system and includes:

    • The title, details and any files you attach.
    • For feedback, your star rating, and your name only if you ask to be quoted.
    • Your email, if you give one, used only to update you on that report.
    • Your iRevive version and macOS version.
    • The activity log, if you leave that box ticked.

    Reports are only used to fix problems and improve iRevive. They're never shared, sold or used for marketing. To have a report deleted, email leon@gilroy.digital.
